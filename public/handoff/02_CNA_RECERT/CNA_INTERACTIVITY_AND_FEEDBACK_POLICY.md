## 1. Purpose

This policy defines the standards for required learner interactivity in all online CNA continuing education (CE) courses offered by CI Institute of Nursing, and establishes the mechanisms by which learner feedback is collected, reviewed, and used for course improvement. CDPH TPRU regulations require that online CE courses include active learner engagement and not function as passive video or document consumption. This policy documents how CI Institute of Nursing meets that requirement.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:
- All online CNA CE courses offered through the CI Institute of Nursing LMS (courses CNA-CE-001 through CNA-CE-012 and any future additions to the online CE catalog).
- All course authors, instructors, and LMS administrators responsible for course design, delivery, and evaluation.
- CDPH reviewers accessing the LMS to evaluate online CE offerings.

---

## 3. Policy Statement

CI Institute of Nursing requires that every online CNA CE course include learner-driven active interactions that go beyond page-turning or document review. Every course must include knowledge checks, scenario-based activities, a final posttest, and a learner feedback mechanism. No CE credit is awarded for passive viewing alone. Active engagement is tracked by the LMS and is a component of the minimum active minutes determination.

---

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 — Required Interactivity Elements Per Course

Each online CNA CE course must include at minimum the following interactive elements:

| Element | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Knowledge Checks (Mid-Course) | Minimum one (1) knowledge check per major content section; minimum three (3) per course |
| Scenario-Based Activity | Minimum one (1) applied care scenario in each course requiring learner response or selection |
| Final Posttest | Required summative posttest; minimum 10 questions; minimum passing score 80% |
| Affidavit Acknowledgement | Required completion acknowledgement activity confirming the learner completed the course independently |
| Learner Feedback Survey | Post-course satisfaction and content feedback survey; optional but offered to all completers |

### 4.2 — Knowledge Check Design

- Knowledge checks must require learner response to advance in the course.
- Knowledge checks use multiple-choice, true/false, or matching item formats.
- Knowledge checks are formative; learners may retry without grade penalty.
- Completion of each knowledge check is logged as a required activity in the LMS.
- Feedback on incorrect knowledge check responses must be available to the learner before advancing.

### 4.3 — Scenario-Based Activity Design

- At least one scenario per course must present a realistic CNA care situation requiring the learner to choose an appropriate response.
- Scenarios must include feedback explaining why each response option is correct or incorrect.
- Scenarios are embedded within Moodle lesson or SCORM activity resources.

### 4.4 — Posttest Interactivity Requirements

- Posttest must be completed in a single session without extended leave-and-return window.
- Posttest questions are drawn from a question bank per course.
- Posttest attempts and scores are logged by the LMS.
- Posttest is gated: learners cannot access it until all prior required activities and minimum active time are met.
- See CNA Posttest and Exam Policy (CNA-POL-004) for full posttest procedures.

### 4.5 — Affidavit Completion

- The affidavit is a required LMS activity that asks the learner to confirm:
  - They completed the course on their own without unauthorized assistance.
  - The identity information in their profile is accurate.
  - They understand this CE activity is being submitted for CNA renewal credit.
- Affidavit completion is timestamped and retained in the learner record.
- Certificate release is blocked until affidavit activity is marked complete.

### 4.6 — Learner Feedback Survey

- A satisfaction and content feedback survey is offered immediately following course completion.
- Survey completion is voluntary and does not affect CE credit or certificate release.
- Survey responses are reviewed by the Program Director at minimum annually.
- Survey results inform course revision decisions.

### 4.7 — Feedback on Survey Findings

- If survey responses indicate a content concern, comprehension gap, or technical issue, the Program Director initiates a course review.
- Material changes to course content require instructor review and a version update logged in the course revision record.
- Learners who complete a revised course receive the updated version upon their next enrollment.

## 5. Responsible Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Course Author / Instructor | Design and embed required interactive elements; review feedback results |
| LMS Administrator | Configure completion requirements for all interactive activities; verify gate logic |
| Program Director | Approve course design; review feedback outcomes; authorize course revisions |
| CDPH Reviewer (external) | Review interactive elements as part of online CE provider approval |

---

## 6. Required LMS Control

- All knowledge checks, scenario activities, affidavit, and posttest must be configured as required activities in the LMS.
- Completion status for each required activity must be individually tracked.
- Posttest must not be accessible until all preceding required activities are complete.
- Affidavit must be required before certificate release conditions are evaluated.
- Feedback survey must be deployed post-completion and results stored in a reviewable format.

---

## 7. Evidence Retained

- LMS activity completion logs for each required interactive element, per learner.
- Posttest attempt records with scores and timestamps.
- Affidavit completion logs with timestamps.
- Learner feedback survey response records (anonymized aggregate at minimum).
- Course revision log documenting changes triggered by feedback.

---

## 8. Recordkeeping and Audit Requirement

- Interactivity completion records are retained for a minimum of five (5) years.
- Annual audit of interactive element configuration and completion gate logic is required.
- Audit findings are documented and retained.
- Changes to interactive element requirements are reviewed by the Program Director before implementation.

---

## 9. Exception Handling

- If a required interactive activity is unavailable due to a technical failure, the LMS administrator must document the outage and affected learners.
- Affected learners are allowed to complete the activity when restored; credit is not released until the activity is completed.
- No exception may waive the posttest, affidavit, or scenario requirements.
- All exceptions are reviewed by the Program Director.
