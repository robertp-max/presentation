## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the standards and procedures that CI Institute of Nursing uses to enforce minimum active participation time requirements in all online CNA continuing education (CE) courses. California CDPH TPRU online CE provider requirements include a minimum active engagement standard as a condition of CE credit. This policy documents how CI Institute of Nursing meets that requirement through LMS timer controls and seat-time enforcement.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:
- All online CNA CE courses offered through the CI Institute of Nursing LMS (CNA-CE-001 through CNA-CE-012 and any future additions).
- All LMS administrators, course authors, and instructors responsible for configuring or monitoring seat-time controls.
- All learners enrolled in online CNA CE courses.

---

## 3. Policy Statement

CI Institute of Nursing enforces a minimum active participation standard of fifty (50) minutes per CE credit hour in all online CNA CE courses. For a two (2) CE hour course, the minimum active time requirement is one hundred (100) minutes. No learner may access the final posttest or receive a certificate of completion until the LMS-tracked active time requirement has been met. Passive viewing time or inactive tab/session time does not count toward this requirement.

---

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 — Active Time Standard

| Credit Hours | Minimum Active Minutes |
|---:|---:|
| 1 | 50 |
| 2 | 100 |
| 3 | 150 |

All current CNA CE courses are 2 CE hours. The minimum active time for each course is 100 minutes.

### 4.2 — What Counts as Active Time

Active time is defined as time during which the learner is demonstrably engaged with course content:
- Reading or scrolling through lesson pages (with session activity indicator).
- Responding to knowledge check questions.
- Completing scenario-based activities.
- Watching required video content (where applicable and verified by video completion tracking).
- Completing the final affidavit.
- Attempting the posttest.

### 4.3 — What Does Not Count as Active Time

The following do not count toward the minimum active time requirement:
- Browser tabs open but idle for more than five (5) consecutive minutes.
- Session time recorded after auto-logout due to inactivity.
- Time spent outside of the course in other applications.
- Time recorded during a failed or timed-out LMS session.

### 4.4 — LMS Timer Configuration

- The LMS records session activity at configurable intervals.
- Inactivity detection is set to a maximum of five (5) minutes before the session is treated as idle.
- The LMS accumulates active time across multiple valid sessions until the minimum is reached.
- The active time counter is course-specific; time in one course does not accrue toward another.

### 4.5 — Posttest Access Gate

- The final posttest is configured with a prerequisite access restriction that requires the minimum active time threshold to be met.
- The LMS enforces this gate automatically; learners who have not met the minimum time will see the posttest as locked.
- LMS administrators cannot override this gate without Program Director authorization and a documented exception record.

### 4.6 — Certificate Release Gate

- Certificate release conditions include verification that active time has met or exceeded the minimum required minutes.
- The certificate gate is enforced independently of the posttest gate; both conditions must be satisfied.

### 4.7 — Session Recording and Log Review

- LMS session logs for each learner are retained per CI Institute of Nursing recordkeeping policy.
- Administrators may review session logs to confirm active time for any learner upon request.
- Session logs are made available to CDPH reviewers as part of provider review access.

## 5. Responsible Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| LMS Administrator | Configure timer controls; audit session logs; enforce gate settings |
| Course Author / Instructor | Verify course content is structured to require active engagement across minimum time |
| Program Director | Approve policy; authorize any override exceptions; review audit findings |
| CDPH Reviewer (external) | Review timer enforcement as part of online CE provider approval |

---

## 6. Required LMS Control

- LMS must track active session time per learner per course.
- Inactivity timeout must be set to five (5) minutes or less.
- Posttest must be configured with a prerequisite condition requiring the minimum active time value.
- Certificate release must include active time verification as a completion condition.
- Session activity logs must be exportable for CDPH review.

---

## 7. Evidence Retained

- Per-learner session activity logs including session start, end, active duration, and inactivity periods.
- Active time accumulation records per course per learner.
- Posttest access event log (confirming timer gate was cleared before access).
- Certificate release event record (confirming all conditions including active time were met).
- Any documented exceptions with authorization and rationale.

---

## 8. Recordkeeping and Audit Requirement

- Session and timer records are retained for a minimum of five (5) years.
- Annual audit of LMS timer configuration and gate enforcement is required.
- Timer audit results are documented and reviewed by the Program Director.
- Any configuration changes to timer settings require Program Director approval and a dated change log entry.

---

## 9. Exception Handling

- If a technical failure causes session time to be inaccurately recorded, the incident must be documented with evidence of the failure.
- The learner affected by a documented technical failure may be allowed to re-access the course for the affected session only, with administrator logging.
- Exceptions must be reviewed and approved by the Program Director.
- No exception may reduce the effective minimum time below the regulatory standard.
