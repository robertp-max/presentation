### CNA-CE-001 — Infection Control and Standard Precautions

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Course Number** | CNA-CE-001 |
| **Course Title** | Infection Control and Standard Precautions |
| **CE Hours** | 2 |
| **Minimum Active Minutes** | 100 |
| **Source CNA Module(s)** | Module 6 (Infection Control) and Module 12 (Emergency Procedures) |
| **Moodle Shell Reference** | `OUTPUT_MOODLE_COURSE_BUILD/CNA_RECERT_MOODLE_BUILD/CNA-CE-001` |
| **Build Spec Reference** | `CNA-CE-001_BUILD_SPEC.md` |
| **Question Bank Reference** | `CNA-CE-001_QUESTION_BANK.md` |

**Learning Objective Summary:**
Apply infection-control standards and identify reportable exposure and prevention actions. Learners will demonstrate proper PPE selection, hand hygiene technique, standard and transmission-based precaution workflows, and correct escalation steps when infection signs are observed.

**Specific Learning Objectives:**
1. Apply standard precautions in routine resident care tasks.
2. Identify when additional transmission-based precautions are needed and notify nursing staff.
3. Demonstrate proper hand hygiene sequence before and after care interactions.
4. Select appropriate PPE for common CNA care activities.
5. Recognize signs and symptoms that require prompt nurse notification.
6. Follow safe linen and waste-handling practices.
7. Explain CNA role boundaries for infection-control decision-making.
8. Document and report infection-related observations accurately.

**Quiz/Posttest Requirement:**
- Required scored posttest; minimum passing score 80%.
- Posttest unlocks only after all required lessons and section knowledge checks are completed and minimum active time (100 minutes) is verified.
- Question pool source: `CNA-CE-001_QUESTION_BANK.md`.

**Certificate Release Requirements:**
- All required lesson/resource activities marked complete.
- Minimum 100 active minutes verified by LMS timer.
- All section knowledge checks submitted.
- Final posttest passed at ≥80%.
- Affidavit activity completed and timestamped.
- Required identity fields captured (including CNA certificate number where configured).
