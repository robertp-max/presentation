## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the recordkeeping requirements for CI Institute of Nursing's online CNA continuing education (CE) program. California CDPH TPRU online CE provider requirements mandate that providers maintain accurate and retrievable records of learner identity, enrollment, completion, and CE credit issuance. This policy defines the categories of records maintained, the retention period, access controls, and procedures for providing records to CDPH upon request.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:
- All online CNA CE courses offered by CI Institute of Nursing (CNA-CE-001 through CNA-CE-012 and any future catalog additions).
- All learner records generated by the CI Institute of Nursing LMS.
- All program administrators responsible for LMS management, record access, and CDPH reporting.
- All institutional records related to course content, approval, and revision history.

---

## 3. Policy Statement

CI Institute of Nursing maintains complete, accurate, and secure records for every learner who enrolls in and completes an online CNA CE course. Records include identity verification data, enrollment confirmation, activity completion logs, seat-time records, posttest scores, affidavit completion, and certificate issuance. All records are retained for a minimum of five (5) years from the date of CE completion. Records are available to CDPH upon request.

---

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 — Categories of Records Maintained

| Record Category | Description | Retention Period |
|---|---|---|
| Learner Identity Records | Full name, CNA certificate number, date of birth, email address | 5 years from CE completion date |
| Enrollment Records | Course name, enrollment date, course ID, learner ID | 5 years from CE completion date |
| Activity Completion Logs | Per-activity completion status, timestamps, attempts | 5 years from CE completion date |
| Seat-Time / Active Time Logs | Session start/end, active time accumulated per course per learner | 5 years from CE completion date |
| Knowledge Check Records | Submission date, attempt log, formative score | 5 years from CE completion date |
| Posttest Records | Attempt date(s), score(s), pass/fail status, attempt number | 5 years from CE completion date |
| Affidavit Records | Completion timestamp, learner confirmation text version | 5 years from CE completion date |
| Certificate Issuance Records | Certificate ID, issue date, course ID, learner ID, CE hours awarded | 5 years from CE completion date |
| Course Revision Records | Version log, change summary, date of revision, approving authority | 5 years from last revision date |
| Provider Approval Records | CDPH correspondence, approval letters, NAC number (when issued) | Permanent or per CDPH requirement |

### 4.2 — LMS as Primary Record System

- The CI Institute of Nursing LMS is the primary system of record for all learner-level records.
- The LMS must be configured to retain records within the system for the full retention period.
- LMS records are accessible to authorized administrators at any time.
- Learner records can be exported in a structured format (CSV or PDF) for CDPH review upon request.

### 4.3 — Backup and Data Integrity

- LMS records are backed up on a regular schedule (at minimum weekly automated backup).
- Backup copies are stored in a secure location separate from the primary LMS environment.
- Administrators verify backup integrity at minimum quarterly.
- Any data loss or corruption event must be reported to the Program Director within 24 hours and documented.

### 4.4 — Record Access Controls

- Access to learner records is restricted to:
  - LMS administrators with a designated administrative role.
  - Program Director.
  - CDPH reviewers (read-only access during the review period via reviewer account).
- Learners may access their own records and certificates through the LMS learner portal.
- Access privileges are reviewed and updated when staff roles change.

### 4.5 — Providing Records to CDPH

- Upon CDPH request, CI Institute of Nursing will provide learner or program records in the format specified by CDPH.
- Records will be provided within the timeframe stipulated in the provider agreement or CDPH directive.
- The Program Director is responsible for coordinating record production in response to CDPH requests.
- All CDPH record requests and the CI Institute of Nursing response are themselves logged and retained.

### 4.6 — Certificate Records

- Each certificate of completion contains: learner full name, CNA certificate number, course title, CE hours, course completion date, and CI Institute of Nursing provider name.
- A record of each certificate issuance is retained in the LMS and linked to the learner record.
- Certificates are accessible to learners through their LMS account for the duration of the retention period.

## 5. Responsible Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| LMS Administrator | Configure record retention settings; manage exports; verify backup integrity |
| Program Director | Oversee policy; coordinate CDPH record requests; approve record access |
| Instructor / Course Author | Maintain course revision logs and content version documentation |
| CDPH Reviewer (external) | Access read-only learner and completion records during review period |

---

## 6. Required LMS Control

- LMS must retain all learner records for a minimum of five (5) years from CE completion date.
- LMS must support full export of required record fields per CDPH review format.
- LMS access logs must record all administrator-level access to learner records.
- LMS must enforce role-based access controls for all record categories.

---

## 7. Evidence Retained

See Section 4.1 for the full table of record categories and retention periods. In addition:
- Record of each CDPH record request and CI Institute of Nursing response.
- Backup verification logs.
- Access control audit logs.
- Course revision logs for all catalog courses.

---

## 8. Recordkeeping and Audit Requirement

- Annual internal audit of record completeness, access controls, and backup integrity is required.
- Audit results are reviewed by the Program Director and documented.
- Any gaps in required records are corrected and the correction is logged.
- This policy is reviewed annually and updated as needed to reflect regulatory changes or system changes.

---

## 9. Exception Handling

- If a learner record is found to be incomplete, the LMS administrator investigates the cause and corrects the record where supporting data exists.
- If a record cannot be reconstructed, the gap is documented with the reason and retained in the exception log.
- No record may be deleted before the end of the required retention period without Program Director authorization and a documented rationale.
