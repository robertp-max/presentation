## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes the procedures and requirements used by CI Institute of Nursing to verify the identity of learners enrolling in and completing online continuing education (CE) courses offered under the California Nursing Assistant (CNA) CE renewal program. Identity verification is a regulatory compliance requirement for online CE providers seeking CDPH TPRU approval under California Health and Safety Code provisions governing CNA renewal education.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:
- All learners enrolled in online CNA CE courses offered through the CI Institute of Nursing learning management system (LMS).
- All program administrators, instructors, and LMS operators responsible for enrollment management, identity field configuration, and completion record review.
- All CNA CE offerings in the CI Institute of Nursing online catalog, including but not limited to courses CNA-CE-001 through CNA-CE-012.

---

## 3. Policy Statement

CI Institute of Nursing requires that each learner's identity be collected, confirmed, and linked to their CE completion record before a certificate of completion is issued. No CE certificate will be released to a learner whose identity has not been verified through the LMS enrollment process. Identity verification is not a one-time check at enrollment only; it is a gate condition tied to certificate release for each individual course completed.

Identity data collected through the LMS is retained in compliance with CI Institute of Nursing recordkeeping policy and made available to CDPH upon request.

---

## 4. Procedure

### 4.1 — Enrollment Identity Collection

At the time of account creation and course enrollment, the LMS requires each learner to provide and confirm the following identity data elements:

| Required Field | Description |
|---|---|
| Full Legal Name | First, middle (if applicable), last name as it appears on government-issued ID |
| CNA Certificate Number | California CNA certificate number issued by CDPH |
| Date of Birth | Used for identity confirmation and record matching |
| Email Address | Primary contact for enrollment confirmation and certificate delivery |
| Employer/Facility Name (optional) | Collected where provided for recordkeeping purposes |

### 4.2 — LMS Identity Field Configuration

- All required identity fields listed in Section 4.1 are configured as mandatory profile fields within the LMS.
- Learners cannot complete enrollment or access course content until all required fields are populated.
- The LMS captures the date and time of field completion and stores this with the learner record.

### 4.3 — Identity Field Verification at Certificate Release

- Before a CE certificate is generated, the LMS completion logic verifies that all required identity fields are populated and non-null.
- Certificates are not released to learner accounts where required fields are absent or incomplete.
- CNA certificate number must be present in the learner record at the time of certificate release.

### 4.4 — Instructor and Administrator Review

- Program administrators conduct periodic audits of identity fields in enrolled learner records.
- Any record missing required identity data is flagged for follow-up prior to certificate release.
- The responsible administrator confirms resolution before overriding completion holds.

### 4.5 — CDPH Access to Identity Records

- Upon CDPH reviewer request, identity records for specific learners or cohorts can be exported from the LMS.
- Export format includes all required fields, enrollment date, completion date, and course identifier.
- Exports are retained per CI Institute of Nursing recordkeeping policy.

## 5. Responsible Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| LMS Administrator | Configure and maintain identity fields; audit records; support exports |
| Program Director | Approve policy; oversee compliance with identity controls |
| Instructor/Course Author | Verify course-level certificate gates include identity field requirements |
| CDPH Reviewer (external) | Review identity records as part of provider approval/audit process |

---

## 6. Required LMS Control

- Identity fields must be configured as required fields in the LMS user profile module.
- Certificate release activity must have a conditional dependency on populated identity fields.
- LMS must log field-completion timestamp for audit purposes.
- System must prevent certificate download for records with missing required identity data.

---

## 7. Evidence Retained

- LMS learner profile records (full name, CNA certificate number, date of birth, email).
- Enrollment date/time stamp.
- Identity field completion log.
- Certificate release event record (tied to learner ID and course ID).
- Audit log of any administrative review or override action.

---

## 8. Recordkeeping and Audit Requirement

- Identity records are retained for a minimum of five (5) years from the date of CE completion.
- Records are stored in a secure LMS environment with access controls limiting access to authorized administrators.
- CI Institute of Nursing will make identity verification records available to CDPH upon request within the timeframe specified in the provider agreement.
- Annual internal audit of identity field configuration and certificate gate enforcement is required.

---

## 9. Exception Handling

- If a learner cannot supply a CNA certificate number due to a pending renewal or typographic error, the learner must contact CI Institute of Nursing administration before course access is granted.
- Exceptions must be documented in the learner record with a brief explanation and administrator initials.
- No exception overrides the requirement for legal name and date of birth fields.
- Exceptions are reviewed by the Program Director before resolution.
